# Loudnight for investors

This page is for people considering an investment. The project README is written for users and judges.

## The ask: $25,000

### Proof it works: one venue, start to finish (Austin, Oct 14)

| Step                | What happened                                                                               | Owner's time                 |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Set up the venue    | Address, two quiet nights, a $400 performer budget, what the room can host                  | 2 minutes                    |
| Plan                | 35 Qloo calls, nearby venues and events checked, 3 nights scored with evidence              | About 3 minutes, automatic   |
| Choose and schedule | "Cider & Alt-Country Wednesdays" (score 100) became 4 dated nights                          | 1 click                      |
| Book performers     | 10 local acts suggested with budget labels; 2 booked, each with a message ready to send     | A few minutes                |
| Promote             | Poster, story and video with both acts' photos, and captions for each network               | Automatic                    |
| Fill the room       | The event page went live; a guest saved a place from a phone without an account             | Under a minute for the guest |
| Run the door        | The ticket was scanned on a second phone; the door screen on a third went from 0 to 2 of 35 | Seconds per guest            |

### What one venue is worth

|                         | Amount                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Price                   | $49 a month (about €45, £39); groups of up to five venues $199                                                                       |
| Cost to serve one venue | Under $1 a month at one plan a month: about $0.30 of AI calls a plan, and Qloo answers are cached and shared by area                 |
| Gross margin            | Above 95% before Qloo's commercial price, which sets the real margin                                                                 |
| Value to the venue      | A venue that breaks even at 35 guests spending $38 each makes about $1,330 on one extra full night: more than two years of Loudnight |
| Paid tickets, later     | A 3% booking fee paid by the guest, capped at $1                                                                                     |

| Use                                | Amount      | What it buys                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| **Total**                          | **$25,000** |                                                                                                                                                                                              |

### Targets for the first twelve months (targets, not results)

| By       | Target                                                                 | Monthly subscription revenue                       |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Month 3  | 30 paying venues in two US cities                                      | $1,470                                             |
| Month 6  | 100 venues in the US and London, paid ticketing live                   | $4,900, plus booking fees                          |
| Month 12 | 300 venues across the US and Europe, first venues in Nairobi and Lagos | $14,700 (about $176,000 a year), plus booking fees |

Booking fees at month 12, as an illustration: 300 venues x 4 nights a month x 40 tickets x $0.30 (3% of a $10 ticket) = $14,400 a month.

**Assumptions:** 4 in 10 venue meetings start a free plan; 1 in 4 free venues upgrade; winning a paying venue costs under $60; fewer than 1 in 20 venues leave each month. The administrator console tracks the inputs from the first month.
