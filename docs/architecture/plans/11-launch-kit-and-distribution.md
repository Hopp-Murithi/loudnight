# Plan 11: Launch kit and distribution

Status: In progress (the ad studio first, see the revision at the end)
Depends on: 10
Phase: 4
Judging criteria: Design (one tap from idea to ready-to-post), Potential Impact (an idea fills nothing until people hear about it)

## Goal

One tap produces everything a night needs to be heard about, written for the audience in their own references: event description, a poster, posts for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Google Business Profile, themed menu specials, a regulars message (SMS and email), a performer promo kit, a partner cross-promo pitch, and an ad targeting brief. Every asset is one tap from copied, downloaded or shared. Nothing posts automatically.

## Brief coverage

"Launch. One click produces the event page with RSVP and QR code, a poster, posts for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Google Business Profile, themed menu specials and an ad targeting brief." Distribution table: event page, social posts, paid ads brief ("fans of X + Y", radius, age band, suggested daily budget), performer promo kit, partner cross-promo ("local brands the audience over-indexes on, with a co-host pitch"), regulars (SMS and email text). "Posters without an image model: designed HTML/SVG templates rendered to PNG with Satori, filled with the concept's name, palette and copy." "In the demo, every asset is one tap from being copied or shared." Cut-list: partner cross-promo (2) and poster generation (4).

## Scope

In: `modules/launch-kit` (asset schemas, generation, storage in `event_nights.assets`, regeneration per asset, edits), poster templates and the Satori render route, the ad brief builder, the launch kit tab on `/venues/[id]/nights/[nightId]`, share and download actions.
Out: the public event page and RSVP (plan 12); direct posting to Meta or Google (roadmap, plan 20).

## Design

### Assets (`launch-kit.schemas.ts`, Zod, stored in `event_nights.assets`)

| Asset               | Fields                                                                                                               | Generated by                                   |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Event description   | title, one line, body (120 to 180 words), what to expect, accessibility note                                         | `AI_MODELS.copy`                               |
| Posts               | per channel: caption, hashtags (Instagram, TikTok), call to action, character limits respected                       | `AI_MODELS.copy`, one call                     |
| Poster              | template id, palette, headline, sub line, date line, QR placement                                                    | Template plus copy                             |
| Menu specials       | 2 to 4 items with name, one line, suggested price band from the average spend                                        | `AI_MODELS.copy`                               |
| Regulars message    | SMS (under 160 characters), email subject and body                                                                   | `AI_MODELS.copy`                               |
| Performer promo kit | short post for the act, story text, link, poster variant                                                             | `AI_MODELS.copy` plus template                 |
| Partner cross-promo | 2 or 3 local brands or places from the fingerprint, co-host pitch each                                               | Qloo brands and places with receipts plus copy |
| Ad brief            | interests list (Qloo affinities mapped to plain interest names), radius, age band, suggested daily budget, run dates | Pure builder plus receipts                     |

- Every model call is grounded: inputs are the concept, the night, the venue's public facts and fingerprint items with receipt ids; a validator rejects any digit that is not a date, time, price from settings, or a receipt value.
- Channel rules in `config/channels.ts` (character limits, hashtag counts, tone notes).
- Ad brief: age band from the demographic skew receipts; radius from settings; daily budget = a share of the talent budget over the run days (pure, tested). Labelled "suggested", with the maths shown.

### Posters

- Add `satori` and `@resvg/resvg-js` (pinned; check build size and that the route runs on the Node runtime). Three templates in `modules/launch-kit/posters/` (Bold, Gig, Supper), sized 1080x1350 (feed) and 1080x1920 (story).
- `GET /api/v1/nights/[id]/poster?size=feed|story` renders PNG, cached by asset hash; public for published nights (the poster appears on the event page), team-only for drafts.
- Fonts bundled from `next/font` sources (Geist) as files in the repo; no remote fetch at render time.

### API

| Method and path                         | Access         | Notes                                               |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| `POST /api/v1/nights/[id]/kit`          | Owner, manager | Generates all assets (streamed progress per asset)  |
| `POST /api/v1/nights/[id]/kit/[asset]`  | Owner, manager | Regenerates one asset                               |
| `PATCH /api/v1/nights/[id]/kit/[asset]` | Owner, manager | Saves an owner edit (kept beside the generated one) |
| `GET /api/v1/nights/[id]/poster`        | As above       | PNG                                                 |

## Screens

- **Launch kit tab** on `/venues/[id]/nights/[nightId]`: "Make the launch kit" when empty; progress list while generating (one line per asset); then asset cards: 1 column at 390, 2 at 768, 3 at 1280 and 1440.
- Each card: channel icon and name, the text in a readable block, character count against the limit, Copy, Share (Web Share on phones), Edit (sheet), Regenerate. Poster card: preview, download feed and story PNGs.
- Ad brief card: interests as chips with receipts, radius, age band, daily budget with "How we got this".
- Partner card: brand or place name with receipt and the pitch.
- States: generation partly failed (failed cards show Retry), all done, edited (badge "Edited by you").

## Revision, Oct 14: the ad studio comes first

The owner asked for posters, banners and video ads for the chosen night that are colourful, uncrowded, not generic, cost almost nothing to make, animate (a slow zoom), and can be pushed to social platforms from the phone. This section replaces the poster and posts parts of the design above. The rest of the kit (menu specials, regulars message, partner pitch, ad brief) stays as planned and comes after.

### What it is

`/venues/[id]/promote`: pick one of the four nights, then a format, a template and a colourway. The ad is drawn live from the night's own facts and can be saved as an image, recorded as a short video, or shared.

### Decisions

- **No image or video model.** Every ad is a designed template drawn on a canvas in the browser from stored facts: the night's name, the venue, the date and start time, and the booked act or the week's twist. It costs no tokens and no server time, and the same code draws the still, the preview and the video.
- **Three templates**, each with one clear idea and at most four pieces of text: Marquee (a full photo with the name over it and the date on a colour band), Block (a field of colour with the photo in a slanted window), Ticket (paper, a giant day number and a tear line, no photo).
- **Four colourways** (Ember, Plum, Lagoon, Sunset), defined once in `config/promote.ts`. Canvas cannot read CSS tokens, so the hex values live there and nowhere else.
- **Four formats**: poster 1080x1350, story 1080x1920, square 1080x1080, banner 1200x628.
- **Video**: six seconds at 30 frames a second, recorded from the canvas with `MediaRecorder`. MP4 where the browser can write it, WebM otherwise. The motion is a slow zoom on the photo, the title rising in line by line, and the date band wiping across. Under reduced motion the preview is the finished still.
- **Photos**: one of the CC0 night photos already in the repo, the owner's own photo (read locally, never uploaded), or the booked act's photo from Qloo, fetched through the app's own image route so the canvas stays exportable. Photos that need a credit (CC BY) are not offered for ads.
- **Sharing**: `navigator.share` with the file and the caption opens the phone's own share sheet, which is how an ad reaches Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp or anything else installed. Without it, the file downloads. Posting through each network's API needs their app review and stays on the roadmap (plan 20).
- **Captions**: built by rule from the same facts for Instagram, Facebook, X and a text message, each inside its limit, with hashtags from the city, the weekday and the night's format. No model call. A model-written variant can come with the rest of the kit.
- **Saved design**: the chosen template, colourway, photo and extra line are stored per night in `event_nights.assets.ad` (`PATCH /api/v1/nights/[id]/ad`, owners and managers), so the event page (plan 12) can show the same poster.
- The server-side poster route, Satori and resvg are not needed for this and are not added.
- **Ticket link and QR code**: an optional https link per night (a ticket seller or an RSVP page). When set, the ad carries a QR code for it with a "Scan for tickets" label, and every caption ends with the link. Drawn with `qrcode-generator` (MIT, no dependencies, pinned). Once the event page exists (plan 12) its address becomes the default link.
- **Redo**: Play it again, Film it again, Refresh the details (re-reads the night from the server) and Start this ad over.

### Tests

Pure parts only: what goes on the ad for a night (with and without a booked act), captions and their limits, hashtags, the animation timeline, text fitting, the video type choice, the design schema, and the save service. Drawing itself is checked on screen.
